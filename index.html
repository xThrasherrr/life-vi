Welcome to LIFE VI

  • Your bars drain as the day goes on. Log meals, water, walks, focus sessions and sleep to refill them.
  • Missions with a deadline raise your wanted level when they run late. Finish them to clear it.
  • Everything stays on this phone. No account, and nothing is uploaded.
